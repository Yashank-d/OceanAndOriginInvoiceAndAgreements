# PDF Design Source of Truth

The generated PDFs must reproduce the intended Parva agreement style.

## General character
Premium, editorial, restrained, formal, highly readable.

This is a professional contract/document, not a brochure.

## Agreement
Target approximately 6 to 8 pages depending on content.

Page 1:
- Small Parva logo
- THE HOUSE OF PARVA
- MASTER CLIENT BOOKING AGREEMENT
- Client/booking summary
- Introductory statement
- Agreement metadata

Body pages:
- Eight numbered sections
- Clear serif section headings
- Readable body copy
- Generous whitespace
- Thin rules
- Consistent margins
- Page numbers
- Header/footer
- Initial boxes on appropriate pages

Final page:
- Spacious signature blocks

## Booking Schedule
1 to 2 pages.
Use clear information blocks with strong hierarchy.

## Invoice
Use:
- Document title
- Client block
- Item table
- Totals
- Payment details
- Footer

## Typography
Use an elegant serif for major titles/headings and a highly readable sans-serif for body/interface text.

Do not use oversized typography that wastes page space.

## Colour
Use warm ivory/off-white as the document base and deep burgundy/wine as restrained accent colour.

Do not introduce arbitrary colours.

## Layout requirements
- Consistent margins
- Proper page breaks
- No clipping
- No overlapping
- No orphaned headings where avoidable
- Tables stay within page width
- Long paragraphs flow naturally
- Signature areas never split awkwardly
- Text remains selectable
- Print-friendly

## Critical
Visually inspect generated PDFs during QA.
Fix layout problems rather than accepting technically valid but visually poor PDFs.
