# Agreement Generator Specification

## Title
MASTER CLIENT BOOKING AGREEMENT

## Fixed legal structure
The master agreement has exactly these eight core sections:

1. Parties & Booking Details
2. Services, Deliverables & Creative Scope
3. Fees, Payment & Booking Confirmation
4. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Unforeseen Circumstances
5. Event, Client & Team Responsibilities
6. Delivery, Albums & Data
7. Copyright, Privacy & Portfolio Usage
8. General Terms & Signatures

The legal wording is fixed source-of-truth content.
Do not rewrite, simplify, paraphrase, invent, remove, or contradict clauses.

## Variable fields

### Client
- Full name
- Address
- Phone
- Email

### Booking
- Booking ID
- Booking date
- Event/shoot type
- Event date
- Event location
- Coverage start
- Coverage end

### Services
- Package name
- Selected services
- Deliverables
- Additional services
- Team/coverage details where required

### Commercial
- Total fee
- Booking advance
- Pre-event payment
- Final payment
- Payment due dates
- Additional charges
- Travel/accommodation charges where applicable

Default payment:
- 50% booking advance
- 30% pre-event
- 20% final

### Delivery
- Expected delivery timeline
- Photography revisions: 2 rounds
- Film revisions: 1 round
- Archival: 3 months for smaller shoots/events, 6 months for weddings/larger productions

### Special terms
- Free text

### Signatures
- Client name
- Client signature
- Client date
- Authorised representative name
- Service provider signature
- Service provider date

## Established commercial/legal principles
Preserve the approved master wording covering:
- Booking confirmation after accepted services, signed agreement and 50% advance
- Final deliverables withheld until outstanding payment is cleared
- RAW/unedited/rejected/duplicate/test/outtake/source files excluded unless agreed
- Revision requests consolidated in writing within 7 days
- Portfolio usage by default with written opt-out before event/shoot
- Personal non-commercial client usage after full payment
- Commercial usage requires written permission
- Parva editorial judgment over selection, composition, editing, colour grading, music, story structure and final presentation
- No guarantee of specific photographs/moments/compositions
- Venue restrictions
- Client/guest professional conduct
- Coverage delays and additional coverage
- Booking-specific travel/accommodation
- Emergency replacement provisions
- Force majeure
- Cancellation and rescheduling
- Data/archival provisions
- Physical/electronic execution
- Governing law and jurisdiction as approved

Do not invent new legal terms.
