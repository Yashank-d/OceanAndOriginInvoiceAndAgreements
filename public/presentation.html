06 // Technical Deep Dive

Vector PDF Agreement Engine & Isolated Scroll

Solved two major UX challenges in contract software: nested scroll wheel trapping and blurry rasterized document previews.

INNOVATION 01

Isolated Scroll Decoupling

Separated the outer viewport scroll container from the internal PDF canvas scroll container using CSS overscroll-behavior: contain and dedicated wheel event traps. The parent web app never stutters or jumps when the user examines multi-page contract terms.

INNOVATION 02

Tri-Document Live Generation

One click generates 3 distinct, production-ready legal artifacts:
• Master Agreement: 8 legal clauses, signatures, and schedule of rights.
• Booking Schedule: Call sheet, crew allocation, and shot list.
• Tax Invoice: Compliant GST milestone tax invoice.
